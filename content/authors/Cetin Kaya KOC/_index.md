<div style="text-align: justify">

Prof. Çetin Kaya Koç is an internationally renowned cryptographic engineer and one of the founders of the field of cryptographic hardware and embedded systems security. He received his BS in Electrical Engineering, summa cum laude, from Istanbul Technical University, and his PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB) in 1988.

Prof. Koç began his academic career at the University of Houston (1988–1992) before joining Oregon State University (1992–2007), where he rose to full professor and founded the Information Security Laboratory. From 1991 to 1997 he was also a member of RSA Laboratories. He was a faculty member of the Department of Computer Science at UCSB from 2008 to 2024, where his Koç Lab hosted many visiting researchers and interns, including several CALAS PhD graduates. He has also held visiting appointments in Turkey, Italy and China.

In 1999, Prof. Koç co-founded, with Prof. Christof Paar, the Conference on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems (CHES), now the premier venue in the field, and served as its programme co-chair from 1999 to 2003. He is the founding Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Cryptographic Engineering (Springer, since 2011), and co-founded the WAIFI and PROOFS workshops and the ASHES conference. He has served as an associate editor of IEEE Transactions on Computers and IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing.

Prof. Koç has authored or edited books including *Cryptographic Algorithms on Reconfigurable Hardware*, *Cryptographic Engineering*, *Open Problems in Mathematics and Computational Science*, *Cyber-Physical Systems Security* and *Partially Homomorphic Encryption*, all published by Springer. He has supervised 23 PhD and 40 MS students. He was elected an IEEE Fellow in 2007 "for contributions to cryptographic engineering" and an IEEE Life Fellow in 2023. His other honours include the Oregon State University Award for Outstanding and Sustained Research Leadership (2001) and the TÜBİTAK International Fellowship for Outstanding Researchers (2020).

Prof. Koç is a long-time advisor of Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung and joined CALAS as a Team Advisor in November 2026.
