Please join us in congratulating Prof. Guan Nan, Prof. Shiqi Wang, and Prof. Haoliang Li on their inclusion in the 2026 World's Top 2% Scientists list.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

The 2026 list, released by the Stanford University team led by Prof. John P. A. Ioannidis in collaboration with Elsevier, recognizes scientists based on Scopus-indexed citation and authorship indicators. According to the City University of Hong Kong (Dongguan) announcement, 41 scholars were selected for the annual scientific-impact list and 29 for the career-long scientific-impact list.

CALAS is especially proud to celebrate this recognition alongside the work of its faculty members, Prof. Shiqi Wang and Prof. Haoliang Li. Their work strengthens the interdisciplinary research environment around intelligent computing, efficient systems, and trustworthy hardware.

Congratulations again to Prof. Guan, Prof. Wang, and Prof. Li. This recognition is a meaningful affirmation of their research impact and their contributions to the wider academic community.

Source: [City University of Hong Kong (Dongguan) announcement](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/cHCOj_CO0sZWQbA59OIL2g).
