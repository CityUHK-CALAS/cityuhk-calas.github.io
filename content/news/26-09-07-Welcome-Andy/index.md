CALAS warmly welcomes Andy Yue Wu, Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS) Fellow from Imperial College London, as he begins his PhD studies with the group.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

We are delighted to welcome **Andy Yue Wu** to CALAS as a new PhD student under the supervision of **Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung**.

Andy received his MSc in Applied Mathematics from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), where he was supervised by Dr. Estefania Loayza Romero, and his BSc in Mathematics and Statistics from Queen Mary University of London. He joins CALAS as a [Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS)](https://cerg1.ugc.edu.hk/hkpfs/index.html) Fellow, a highly competitive award established by the Research Grants Council of Hong Kong to attract outstanding PhD students from around the world.

His research interests include efficient edge AI, hardware–software co-design, and RISC-V–based systems. Although he has only recently arrived in Hong Kong, Andy is already well known to the group: he has regularly joined CALAS meetings over the past year, and his commitment to the lab has been clear from the start.

A warm welcome to Andy as he begins his PhD with CALAS. We look forward to the work he will do with the group.

</div>
