Congratulations to Dr. Ashraf on the acceptance of his full paper, **"SR-MCUR: A Scalable and Robust CUR Decomposition Method for Large-Scale Matrix Approximation,"** in *Information Sciences* (Impact Factor: 6.0) after two rounds of revisions.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

This is a well-deserved recognition of Dr. Ashraf's research on scalable and robust CUR decomposition for large-scale matrix approximation. The paper was accepted following two rounds of revisions.

Dr. Ashraf thanks Prof. Ray and Prof. Patrick for their guidance and support, and all CALAS members for their encouragement. Special thanks go to co-author Dr. Sanka for his invaluable input in strengthening this work.

Congratulations again to Dr. Ashraf, and we look forward to more good news from the group!
