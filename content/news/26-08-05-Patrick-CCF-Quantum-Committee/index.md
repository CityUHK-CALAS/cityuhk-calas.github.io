Prof. Patrick Hung has been elected to the Special Committee of the China Computer Federation (CCF) Quantum Computing Association.

<!--more-->

CALAS is pleased to announce that **Prof. Patrick Hung** has been elected to the Special Committee of the **China Computer Federation (CCF) Quantum Computing Association**.

This appointment provides an important opportunity to strengthen engagement with China's quantum computing community. Prof. Patrick and Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung look forward to building connections between CALAS and leading quantum computing companies and research groups in China, fostering future academic and industry collaboration.

CALAS extends its warmest congratulations to Prof. Patrick on this appointment and wishes him every success in his new role.
