Congratulations to Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung, who will serve on the Program Committee of IEEE HOST 2027.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

CALAS is pleased to announce that Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung will serve on the Program Committee of the IEEE International Symposium on Hardware Oriented Security and Trust (HOST) 2027. The symposium will be held from 3 to 6 May 2027 in Washington, DC, USA.

IEEE HOST is a leading international forum for research and development in hardware-based security and trust. Its technical programme brings together researchers and practitioners working on hardware security, assurance, trusted computing, and related emerging technologies.

Prof. Ray's appointment reflects his continued contribution to the international hardware security community. We look forward to his service in supporting a strong technical programme for HOST 2027.

For more information, visit the [IEEE HOST 2027 website](https://host.conferences.computer.org/2027/).
