CALAS warmly welcomes Prof. Çetin Kaya Koç, co-founder of the CHES conference, as a Team Advisor.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

CALAS is delighted to welcome [Prof. Çetin Kaya Koç](/author/prof.-cetin-kaya-koc/) to the team as a CALAS Team Advisor. Prof. Koç, originally from Turkey and long based in California, USA, has been a long-time advisor to Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung. On joining, he said he was privileged to be part of the group.

Prof. Koç is one of the founders of cryptographic engineering as a research field. In 1999 he co-founded, with Prof. Christof Paar, the Conference on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems (CHES), which has become the premier international venue for hardware security and cryptographic implementation research. He is also the founding Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Cryptographic Engineering, an IEEE Life Fellow, and the author of influential books including *Cryptographic Engineering* and *Partially Homomorphic Encryption*. Many in our group have learned from the articles of Prof. Koç and his students.

The connection between Prof. Koç and CALAS goes back many years. During his time on the faculty of the University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB), many CALAS PhD graduates interned and took part in research exchanges in his lab. We look forward to his guidance on our work in hardware security, cryptographic hardware and computer arithmetic.

Please join us in warmly welcoming Prof. Koç to CALAS!

</div>
