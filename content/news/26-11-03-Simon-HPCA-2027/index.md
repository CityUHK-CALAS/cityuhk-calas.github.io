Congratulations to Simon Lyu and his co-authors on the acceptance of their paper at IEEE HPCA 2027!

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

We are delighted to congratulate Simon Shengzhe Lyu and the research team on their paper, **"AdaLLM: Decoupling Compute and Bandwidth for Multi-LLM Serving,"** accepted at the 2027 IEEE International Symposium on High-Performance Computer Architecture (HPCA).

The paper explores how multiple large language models can share hardware resources more efficiently. AdaLLM separates the allocation of compute and memory bandwidth and uses a demand-driven scheduler to adapt resources to different workloads. The team demonstrates the architecture with an FPGA prototype and evaluates it on a range of co-located language models.

The work benefited from feedback received during an earlier submission to MICRO, highlighting the value of constructive reviews and persistence in research. Congratulations to Simon and all the co-authors on this achievement!

</div>
