Congratulations to Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung, who will continue to serve as the IEEE Region 10 Student Activities Chair (SAC) in 2027.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

CALAS is pleased to share that Prof. Ray will continue his service as the IEEE Region 10 Student Activities Chair (SAC) for the coming year. This role supports student activities and engagement across IEEE Region 10, which covers the Asia-Pacific region.

Prof. Ray's continued appointment reflects his commitment to advancing student development, professional exchange, and the next generation of technology leaders. He encourages young members of the community to take on volunteer and leadership roles, build connections through service, and learn through active participation.

Congratulations to Prof. Ray on this continued appointment. We look forward to his contributions to IEEE Region 10 and the wider student community in 2027.

</div>
