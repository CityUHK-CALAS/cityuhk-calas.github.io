We are pleased to announce that Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung has been appointed as a Technical Program Committee (TPC) member for ASP-DAC 2027, the 32nd Asia and South Pacific Design Automation Conference.

This appointment recognizes Prof. Cheung's continued contribution to the design automation research community and strengthens CALAS's engagement with leading international research in electronic design automation, computer architecture, and hardware systems.
