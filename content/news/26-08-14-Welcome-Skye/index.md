CALAS is delighted to welcome Skye, an incoming Master student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at City University of Hong Kong, as our newest team member.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

Skye graduated from Shenzhen University, where she majored in Biomedical Engineering, and will commence her studies in the Electrical and Electronic Engineering programme at CityU this September. She joins the research group of Prof. Ray Cheung.

Skye brings with her three years of laboratory research experience, during which she led hardware development projects and earned national-level competition awards, including first and second prizes. Her internship at Chaihuo Maker Space provided her with hands-on experience in open-source hardware and overseas community management, and she also organized an AI-hardware maker camp for young students.

We are pleased to have Skye on board and look forward to her contributions to the team. Welcome aboard, Skye!

</div>
