We are pleased to share that **Jimmy**, a PhD student from Tianjin University, will present his paper at the **IEEE SMC 2026** conference in Bellevue, Washington, USA.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

Jimmy will deliver an oral presentation of his paper, **"Efficient Progressive Pre-training of Language Models by Aligning Model Growth with Dataset Complexity,"** at the IEEE International Conference on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics (SMC 2026), taking place from October 4–6, 2026, in Bellevue, Washington, USA.

This year's conference is centered on the theme **"Human-Centric Intelligence: Shaping the Digital Future"**, highlighting research that advances intelligent systems with a focus on human-centered design and impact.

We wish Jimmy a fruitful presentation and a great trip to IEEE SMC 2026!

</div>
