CALAS warmly welcomes Jeffrey Jinfa Hong, who completed his MSc in Electrical Engineering at CityUHK and now begins his PhD with the group.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

We are delighted to welcome **Jeffrey Jinfa Hong** to CALAS as a new PhD student under the supervision of **Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung**.

Jeffrey received his BEng in Electronic Engineering from Fuzhou University in 2022, and then completed his [MSc in Electrical Engineering](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/) at City University of Hong Kong in 2023. After finishing his Master's degree, he continued with CALAS as a researcher and now takes up this PhD position in the group — a CityUHK Master's graduate staying on for doctoral study.

His research focuses on high-efficiency cryptographic hardware design and algorithm-hardware co-design, including hardware acceleration for post-quantum cryptography. He is already a familiar member of the lab, and this PhD marks the next stage of his work with CALAS.

A warm welcome to Jeffrey as he begins his PhD with CALAS. We look forward to the work he will do with the group.

</div>
