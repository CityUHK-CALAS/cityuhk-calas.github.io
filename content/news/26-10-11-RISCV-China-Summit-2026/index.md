RISC-V Summit China 2026 will take place from October 18 to 20 at the Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center (Futian).

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

All CALAS members are encouraged to note the upcoming sixth RISC-V China Summit, a major annual gathering for the open hardware and RISC-V community. The three-day event begins with tutorials and working meetings on October 18, followed by the opening ceremony and main forum on October 19. Seven technical forums will be held on October 20, covering AI computing, high-performance computing, software ecosystems, automotive electronics, chip design and verification, industry-academia collaboration, and embedded systems.

The summit closely connects with CALAS's work on RISC-V processor architectures, secure and energy-efficient computing, hardware-software co-design, cryptographic hardware, and embedded IoT systems. It provides an important opportunity to follow advances across the RISC-V ecosystem and to exchange ideas with researchers, developers, and industry partners.

For event information and registration, please refer to the [RISC-V Summit China 2026 announcement](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/8yMOaEGgLP52SGcMtKdQ4w).
