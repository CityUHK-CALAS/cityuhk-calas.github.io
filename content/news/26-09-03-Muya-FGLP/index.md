Congratulations to KAPYA Zachariah Muya on being selected for the CityUHK–Cambridge Future Global Leaders Programme (FGLP)! We are so proud of his success.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

**Muya** (Zachariah Muya Kapya), a CALAS undergraduate in the Bachelor of Engineering in Computer and Data Engineering, has been selected for the [Future Global Leaders Programme (FGLP)](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/en/media/news/2025/12/23/future-global-leaders-programme), a year-long CityUHK–Cambridge programme on future leadership and applying that knowledge to real-world problems. The programme includes a two-week residency at the University of Cambridge later this month.

CityUHK is the first international partner of Lucy Cavendish College's FGLP. As Muya clarified, this is a different programme from the CityUHK–Lucy Cavendish College Taught Master's Degree Scholarship.

Muya thanks Prof. Ray and the CALAS team, and is glad to have learnt a lot from everyone while continuing to keep learning. He also plans to apply for the Cambridge Master's and will keep learning from the group along the way.

Congratulations again to Muya — we are proud of this achievement and wish him a rewarding FGLP experience at Cambridge!
