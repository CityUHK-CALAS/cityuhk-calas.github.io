Congratulations to Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung, who has been invited to serve on the Program Committee of ARC 2027.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

CALAS is pleased to announce that Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung has been invited to join the Program Committee of the 23rd International Symposium on Applied Reconfigurable Computing (ARC 2027). The symposium will be held at Humboldt University of Berlin in Berlin, Germany, from 31 March to 2 April 2027.

ARC is a well-established international forum for research on reconfigurable computing. It brings together researchers and practitioners working on FPGA architectures, hardware acceleration, design tools, and emerging applications of reconfigurable systems.

Prof. Ray also served on the ARC Program Committee for 2026, and this renewed invitation reflects his continued contribution to the reconfigurable computing community. We look forward to his service in supporting a strong technical programme for ARC 2027.
