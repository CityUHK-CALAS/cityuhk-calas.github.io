Congratulations to **Prof. Winston**, Haoyang, and the team on the acceptance of their MAYO GPU paper for **CHES 2026**, Volume 4 (online).

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

We are pleased to announce that the paper **"High-Throughput GPU Design and Implementation of MAYO with Matrix Computation Reordering"** has been accepted by IACR Transactions on Cryptographic Hardware and Embedded Systems (TCHES) and will be presented at **CHES 2026**, Volume 4 (online).

The authors are Haoyang Chen, Junhao Huang, Shutong Jin, Ray C. C. Cheung, Donglong Chen, and Wangchen Dai, with affiliations at Sun Yat-sen University, Singapore Management University, City University of Hong Kong, and Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University. The work presents a high-throughput GPU design and implementation of MAYO, a post-quantum digital signature scheme, with matrix computation reordering.

This acceptance follows the team's earlier TCHES minor revision in June 2026. Congratulations to **Prof. Winston** and **Haoyang**, and to CALAS collaborators **Shutong Jin (Claire)** and **Prof. Ray**, on this achievement.
