Congratulations to Leo — good news: **"Compiler-Aware Heterogeneous Graph Learning for HLS QoR Prediction and Design Space Exploration"** has been accepted by **IEEE TCAD**, a CCF Class A journal!

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

This paper has been accepted by the [IEEE Transactions on Computer-Aided Design of Integrated Circuits and Systems (IEEE TCAD)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=43). IEEE TCAD publishes research on computer-aided design of integrated circuits and systems, covering methods, models, algorithms, and design tools for system-level, physical, and logical design.

The authors are Yanlong Huang (Leo), Jierui Liu (Jerry), Yuhan She (Henry), Rongliang Fu, Hong Yan, Tsung-Yi Ho, and Ray C. C. Cheung. Well done to Leo, Jerry, Dr. Henry (Huawei Hong Kong EDA Lab), and Dr. Rongliang (now in an academic position on the mainland) for their major efforts on this work.

Leo thanks Prof. Ray, Prof. Patrick, and all CALAS members. The paper took almost half a year, and the feedback and discussions along the way were essential to this result.

Congratulations again to Leo and the team on this well-deserved acceptance!
