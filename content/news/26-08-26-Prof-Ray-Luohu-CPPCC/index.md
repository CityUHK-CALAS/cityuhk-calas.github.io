We are delighted to share that Prof. Ray Cheung Chak-chung (张泽松) has been named a member of the 7th Shenzhen Luohu District Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC).

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

According to the official announcement issued by the Shenzhen Luohu District Committee of the CPPCC, the membership list of the 7th Committee has been adopted and published. Prof. Ray is listed among the Hong Kong members under the Specially Invited Persons category.

This appointment reflects Prof. Ray's continued public service and his contributions to academic and professional exchange between Hong Kong and Shenzhen.

Congratulations to Prof. Ray on this appointment.

</div>
