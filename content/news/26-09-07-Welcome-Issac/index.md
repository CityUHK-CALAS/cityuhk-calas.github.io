CALAS warmly welcomes Issac Chau, a CityUHK Local Talent Scheme awardee from HKUST with first-class honours, as he begins his PhD with the group.

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

We are delighted to welcome **Issac Chau** (Chau King Tsun) to CALAS as a new PhD student under the supervision of **Prof. Ray C. C. Cheung**.

Issac received his BEng in Computer Engineering from [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.hkust.edu.hk/) in 2025 with first-class honours. He joins City University of Hong Kong as a [Local Talent Scheme (LTS)](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/) awardee for 2026/27, a competitive scheme that supports outstanding local students to pursue PhD study while developing their research as a Research Assistant.

His research interests include hardware architecture and cryptographic hardware acceleration. A warm welcome to Issac as he begins his PhD with CALAS. We look forward to the work he will do with the group.
