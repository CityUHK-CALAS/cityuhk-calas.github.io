Congratulations to Wilson Wong and the GABES team on their U.S. patent approval and their continued innovation in smart fire safety!

<!--more-->

<div style="text-align: justify">

CALAS is delighted to celebrate this milestone for Wilson, co-founder and CTO of [GABES Limited](https://www.gabes.com.hk/en/about-us), and his team. Their work brings together **fire science and artificial intelligence** to advance intelligent fire safety and evacuation solutions.

GABES combines expertise in fire safety engineering with AI and IoT technologies. Its AlphaNexus system uses intelligent fire detection and dynamic evacuation guidance to help people respond to fire emergencies.

The team previously received the [Hong Kong ICT Awards 2025 Silver Award](/news/25-11-28-wilson-company-award/) for its smart building emergency system. This latest milestone is another encouraging step in translating research and engineering into practical solutions for safer communities.

Congratulations again to Wilson and the entire GABES team!
